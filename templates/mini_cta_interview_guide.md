# Mini-CTA Interview Guide (for K10 diagnostic / K12 meta-cognitive)

A lightweight Critical Decision Method walkthrough, used **after** an event (never in-flow under
pressure). Capture descriptive fidelity first; judgement about use comes later, at Tier-2.

1. **Timeline:** Walk me through what happened, start to finish.
2. **Decision point:** Where did you make the key call?
3. **Cues:** What did you notice that others might have missed?
4. **Knowledge:** What did you know that told you what those cues meant?
5. **Options:** What else could it have been? How did you rule those out?
6. **Boundaries:** When would this reasoning mislead you?
7. **Novice contrast:** What would a less experienced colleague have done?

Do **not** ask the person to justify their performance. Record both their words and any
model-drafted summary; the worker confirms the summary.
